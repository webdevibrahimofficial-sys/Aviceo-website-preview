Aviceo Marketing

A connected workspace for the people who run marketing and customer operations. The product is in development, with commercial availability still to be established.

Executive Workspace

An operational view of priorities, approvals and outcomes is being designed for owners and teams.

Campaign Studio

Campaign planning and readiness checks are being developed. The workflow is intended to help teams review assets, audiences and permissions before approving execution.

Inbox and conversations

An internal workflow brings messages and follow-up tasks into a shared context. External channel integrations are still being developed and tested.

Lead management and appointments

The development workflow links conversation context to lead qualification, approvals and booking steps.

Knowledge management

Teams are intended to organize approved reference material, files and catalog information for use in their workflows.

Approvals and reporting

Approval steps are under development. Internal reporting work calculates business metrics from records so teams can trace how outcomes were measured.

AI decision support

Planned decision support will use authorized business context to explain signals and propose next steps. People retain authority over sensitive actions.
