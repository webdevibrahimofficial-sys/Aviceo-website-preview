Building for a connected business

Aviceo is an emerging technology project building AI-powered business management and marketing intelligence, with an initial focus on Egypt and Arabic-speaking markets.

The problem

When teams work across disconnected inboxes, campaign tools and customer records, decisions lose the context that makes them useful.

The product philosophy

Aviceo Marketing is the first product in development. The goal is to connect work with the evidence each team needs to understand what happened and decide what comes next.

Responsible automation

Teams should know what informed a recommendation, which permissions apply and when a person has to approve a decision.

Built for the region

Arabic is part of the experience from the start. We want the language, workflows and product guidance to work naturally for the businesses we aim to serve.
