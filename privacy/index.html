Privacy Policy

This policy describes the public Aviceo website, email inquiries and the scope of the product under development. The website is operated by the team responsible for the Aviceo technology project. You can reach the project operator at the contact address below.

Effective date November 26, 2026

aviceo@outlook.com

Website visitors

The site serves static pages. The hosting service may process IP addresses, request paths, browser information and timestamps to deliver the site and protect its infrastructure. Theme and language preferences are stored locally in your browser. The site uses no analytics, advertising tags or tracking cookies.

Email inquiries and early access

When you email us, your email provider and our Outlook mailbox process your sender address, message and any details you choose to include. The project uses this information to respond, evaluate early-access interest and handle privacy, deletion or security requests. Please share only information needed for the request.

Product development and data sources

Aviceo Marketing is in development. The public website creates no product accounts and receives no Meta Platform Data. Authorized development or future product workflows may involve account information from workspace users, customer and lead records supplied by a business, communications from enabled channels, and campaign or advertising information from authorized services.

Meta and other authorized integrations

Eligible testing or future integrations may use approved scopes to read campaign metrics, business asset identifiers, permitted messages or submitted lead information. Access depends on the user’s authorization, applicable provider terms and permissions. Meta Business Verification and App Review are pending. Authorized owners can revoke access through the provider where available. See Data Deletion for records already retained.

AI processing and sharing

The public website sends no visitor data to an AI service. In the product vision, AI would interpret selected authorized information for decision support. The project uses hosting and mail services for the public site and email workflow. Provider infrastructure may process data across borders. Product processing arrangements and disclosures will be reviewed before onboarding.

Retention, security and rights

Retention depends on the purpose of the record, applicable obligations and technical constraints such as backups. You may contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion or other applicable rights. To protect account and inquiry information, we may verify the request before acting. Use the Data Deletion instructions for deletion requests.

Audience and updates

The site is directed at business users and is not intended for children. Policy changes will be posted here with the applicable effective date.
