Data Deletion Instructions

Email the Aviceo project to request deletion of information held about you or an authorized business account. Use the email action below to set the subject to Aviceo Data Deletion Request.

aviceo@outlook.com

1. Send the request

Write from the email associated with the inquiry, account or integration where possible. Identify the relevant workspace or connected platform if applicable, and briefly describe the data you want removed. Include only details needed to locate the record. Keep passwords, access tokens and identity documents out of your request.

2. Verify authority

We review the sender and request details against the records we can verify. If follow-up is needed, we ask for the minimum additional context and may verify authorization through an existing account owner or established contact channel before releasing or deleting data.

3. Disconnect access where available

If you administer the connected business, review its provider’s connected apps, integrations or business permissions settings and revoke Aviceo access where listed. Revoking access prevents future authorized retrieval. Data already retained requires a separate deletion request.

4. Review, deletion and status

We identify records within verified scope, review any retention obligations, and remove eligible data under our control. Records subject to a legal obligation or retained in backups may have a different deletion schedule. We reply by email with the outcome, applicable limitations or pending steps.
